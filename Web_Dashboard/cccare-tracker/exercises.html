Exercise List

These are the exercises the camera can track, and the mistakes it looks for.

Exercise Usual sets x reps Common mistakes
Sit-to-stand 2 x 10 Knees caving inward, leaning too far forward
Mini squat 3 x 10 Knees caving inward, heels lifting off the floor
Wall push-up 2 x 10 Hips sagging, elbows flaring out
Bicep curl 2 x 10 Swinging the body, elbows drifting forward
Front arm raise 2 x 10 Shrugging the shoulders, arms not reaching shoulder height
Heel raise 3 x 12 Heels not lifting fully, swaying side to side
